Ride-Share Pricing Analysis

Why do Uber and Lyft fares differ? Across 637,976 cleaned trips in Databricks, a linear regression isolated surge pricing as the dominant driver, at roughly $22 per unit increase. The two were statistically indistinguishable on price per mile (p = 0.807), reframing Lyft's opportunity as operational rather than pricing.

PySpark, SQL, Databricks, linear regression
